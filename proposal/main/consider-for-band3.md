# Consider for Band 3

Select this if your program is suitable for the relaxed conditions associated with a Band 3 time award.

Unless your program absolutely needs the very best observing conditions that Mauna Kea or Cerro Pachon has to offer, 
relaxing your cloud cover, image quality, sky brightness, water vapor, or elevation or airmass requirements can greatly 
increase the chances of the program's getting data; see the [Advice for Band 3 programs](https://www.gemini.edu/observing/resources/advice).
