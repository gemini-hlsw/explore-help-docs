# Total Time

A Large Program runs over several semesters, so its proposal carries two time requests: the time
needed for the observations currently defined, and the time needed for the program as a whole.
**Total** is the second of these. It is the only time figure in this panel that you enter by hand;
every other figure is calculated from the observations included in the proposal and cannot be
edited.

Enter the total request in hours, up to two decimal places. Values must lie between 0 and 1000;
anything outside that range is rejected and the field is marked as invalid.

The **Minimum %** beside Total is the smallest share of that request that would still make the
program worth carrying out, and the hours shown next to it are that percentage of Total. It is
a separate value from the **Minimum %** at the top of the panel, which applies to the time
calculated from the observations.

### The calculated rows

*Max Time* and *Min Time* are the longest and shortest execution times of the observations
included in the proposal. When the two are equal a single *Prog. Time* row is shown instead. If no
observations are included, the panel shows "No observations included in proposal." rather than a
time.

Total is shown for **Large Program** proposals only. Every other proposal type requests time for
a single semester, taken from the observations alone.
