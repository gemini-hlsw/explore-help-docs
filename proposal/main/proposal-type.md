# Regular Proposal Type

| Type | Observing |
|---|---|
| Queue | Gemini staff carry out the observations on your behalf, fitting them into the nightly queue as the requested conditions occur. Most programs use this mode. |
| Classical | You, or someone you designate, are responsible for the observing during nights scheduled for your program, and you take whatever conditions those nights bring. |

See the Gemini [observing modes page](https://www.gemini.edu/observing/phase-i) for guidance on
which to choose.

### Changing the type

Switching between Queue and Classical keeps the work you have already done. The **Minimum %**,
the partner splits, the exchange partner, and the **JWST Synergy**, **US Long Term** and
**AEON / Multi-Facility** settings all carry over unchanged.

**Consider for Band 3** applies to Queue proposals only. It disappears when you switch to
Classical, and reappears set to *Yes* when you switch back, so check it before submitting if
you had set it to something else.
