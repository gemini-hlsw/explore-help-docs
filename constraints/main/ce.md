# Cloud Extinction

The cloud extinction describes the maximum allowed attenuation due to clouds **on average** over the duration of the observation.

Extinction | Conditions   | Percentile
:--------: | :----------: | :--------:
0.0 mag    | Photometric  | 50
< 0.1 mag  | Mostly Clear | 55
< 0.3 mag  | Thin Cirrus  | 70
< 0.5 mag  | Cirrus       | 75
< 1.0 mag  | Cloudy       | 80
< 2.0 mag  | Cloudier     | 95
< 3.0 mag  | Thick Clouds | 100

Note that with optical-wavelength observations clouds primarily attenuate the astronomical source, and additional integration time can compensate.
However, in the infrared clouds also emit, causing the infrared background to vary, making sky subtraction difficult.
