# Flamingos-2 Decker

The decker is the mechanical mask that blocks the parts of the field not needed for the current
observing mode, matched automatically to the **FPU** you choose: *Imaging* for the pinhole masks
used in acquisition, *Long Slit* for a long-slit FPU, and *MOS* once a custom mask is in use.

This field is shown as a read-only value for most users, since it follows directly from the FPU.
Gemini staff can override it directly when a non-standard combination is needed.
