# Flamingos-2 Disperser

The grism used for the spectroscopy, chosen from Flamingos-2's three gratings:

| Disperser | Resolving power | Band |
|---|---|---|
| R1200JH | R ≈ 1200 | J + H |
| R1200HK | R ≈ 1200 | H + K |
| R3000   | R ≈ 3000 | J or H or K |

A reset icon appears beside the field once it differs from the configuration's default, to
revert back to it.

See the [Flamingos-2 grisms page](https://www.gemini.edu/instrumentation/flamingos-2/components#Grisms)
for further detail on each grism's coverage and resolution.
