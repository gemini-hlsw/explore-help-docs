# Flamingos-2 FPU

The long-slit focal plane unit, chosen by slit width from 1 to 8 pixels. 
A narrower slit gives higher spectral resolution at the cost of light lost outside the slit; 
a wider slit gathers more light but blurs the spectrum.

A reset icon appears beside the field once it differs from the configuration's default, to revert back to it.

See the [Flamingos-2 components page](https://www.gemini.edu/instrumentation/flamingos-2/components)
for the slit widths in arcsec at the Flamingos-2 focal plane scale.
