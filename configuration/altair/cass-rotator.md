# Cass Rotator

Whether the Cassegrain rotator tracks the sky's rotation during the exposure:

| Mode | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Following | Rotates to track the sky, keeping the field orientation fixed. The normal choice for most observations. |
| Fixed | Does not rotate; the field rotates on the detector over the course of the exposure. |

See the [Altair page](https://www.gemini.edu/instrumentation/altair) for how this interacts with
the adaptive optics correction.
