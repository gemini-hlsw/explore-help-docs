# GMOS Gratings

The grating used for the spectroscopy. Each grating has its own ruling density, dispersion,
simultaneous wavelength coverage and blaze wavelength, which together with the central wavelength
determine the resolution and range of the resulting spectrum.

A reset icon appears beside the field once it differs from the configuration's default, to
revert back to it.

See the [GMOS gratings page](https://www.gemini.edu/instrumentation/gmos/components#Gratings)
for each grating's specifications.
