# FPU

The long-slit focal plane unit, chosen by slit width. A narrower slit gives higher spectral
resolution at the cost of light lost outside the slit; a wider slit gathers more light but
blurs the spectrum.

A reset icon appears beside the field once it differs from the configuration's default, to
revert back to it.

See the [GMOS FPU page](https://www.gemini.edu/instrumentation/gmos/components) for the slit
widths in arcsec.
