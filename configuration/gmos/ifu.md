# IFU

Which of the IFU's two pseudo-slits to use:

| Option | Field |
|---|---|
| IFU 2 Slits | Both slits, the full field. |
| IFU Right Slit (red) | Only the right-hand slit, half the field. |
| IFU Left Slit (blue) | Only the left-hand slit, half the field. |

Using a single slit halves the field of view in exchange for a smaller detector footprint and
less time spent reading out. Use Two Slits unless your target fits within a single slit's field
and you have a reason to prefer one slit over the other.

A reset icon appears beside the field once it differs from the configuration's default, to
revert back to it.
