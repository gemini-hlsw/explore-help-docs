# GMOS MOS Spatial Offsets

The telescope offsets executed over the course of the sequence.

The default for MOS is to not use spatial offsets, however, you may add steps as needed, 
and each position has its own offset (in arcsec) and a guiding toggle for whether the guiding is active for that step.
Use the ✚ button to add another position and the trash icon to remove one; the first position cannot be removed.
