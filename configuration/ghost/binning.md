# GHOST Binning

How many pixels are combined on this detector, as spectral × spatial, from 1×1 up to 4×4.
Binning more pixels together increases signal-to-noise per combined pixel and reduces readout
time, at the cost of spectral and/or spatial resolution.

Blue and Red are binned independently. A reset icon appears beside the field once it differs
from the default, to revert back to it.
