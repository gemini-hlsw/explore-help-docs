# GNIRS FPU

The focal plane unit, which differs by observing mode:

* **Long slit**: a slit width from 0.10″ to 1.0″. A narrower slit gives higher spectral resolution
  at the cost of light lost outside the slit; a wider slit gathers more light but blurs the
  spectrum.
* **IFU**: a resolution, **Low** (3.15″ field) or **High** (1.25″ field, finer sampling). The
  smaller High-resolution field trades field of view for finer spatial sampling.

A reset icon appears beside the field once it differs from the configuration's default, to
revert back to it.
