# GNIRS Camera

The camera used, in two independent choices:

| Choice | Options | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Length | Long / Short | Long gives a finer pixel scale (0.05″/pixel) for higher spatial resolution; Short gives a coarser scale (0.15″/pixel) for a wider field. |
| Colour | Blue / Red | Which of the paired cameras at that pixel scale is used. |

Long and Short cameras are never mixed within an observation. A reset icon appears beside the
field once it differs from the configuration's default, to revert back to it.
