# GNIRS Acquisition

Lets you override the automatic acquisition sequence: its **Type**, the sky offset a Faint
target's acquisition uses, and the acquisition **Filter**. All default to values chosen for you
from the science configuration and target brightness, and the panel starts collapsed since most
observations do not need to change them.

See **GNIRS Acquisition Type** for what each type does, and **Acquisition Filter** for how the
filter is chosen automatically.
