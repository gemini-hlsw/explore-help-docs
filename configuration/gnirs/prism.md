# GNIRS Prism

Whether the spectrum is cross-dispersed, recording several spectral orders at once instead of
just one:

| Prism | Effect |
|---|---|
| Mirror | No cross-dispersion: a single order, the normal long-slit mode. |
| SXD | Short cross-dispersion: several orders recorded together, with the Short camera. |
| LXD | Long cross-dispersion: several orders recorded together, with the Long camera. |

Cross-dispersion trades a wider total spectral range, across the recorded orders, for a more
complex spectrum to reduce. A reset icon appears beside the field once it differs from the
configuration's default, to revert back to it.
