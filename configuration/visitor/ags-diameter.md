# AGS Diameter

The diameter, in arcsec, of the circular region that must remain free of vignetting by the
peripheral wavefront sensor arm. The Automatic Guide Star (AGS) service selects the optimal guide
star while ensuring the arm does not obstruct any part of this region.

This is not the allowable distance of the guide star from the science target — it is the size of
the unvignetted field that must be preserved while positioning the guide probe.
