# Minimum FoV

The smallest instrument field of view, in arcseconds, that a configuration must offer to appear
in the table of matching configurations below. Leave it blank to see every configuration
regardless of field of view.

Use this when your imaging needs the field of view to cover more than one target, or a specific
amount of surrounding sky, so that configurations too narrow for the purpose are filtered out of
the table rather than needing to be checked by eye.
